# Natural Gas

C-NGMM is a natural gas market model patterned on the formulation of EIA's Natural Gas Market
Module in NEMS. It clears a multi-regional market over production, pipeline flow, storage, and
LNG trade against sectoral demand, returning regional prices as duals of the market-balance
constraint.
